## Gap Inversion → Hourly Continuation

NQ gaps, inverts back through the prior close, and the first hour closes against the gap — does the next hour follow through? 10-year study (2016–2026, 33,739 hourly pairs).

- Baseline: an average hour makes a **lower low 43.6%** / **higher high 48.6%** of the time.
- **Bearish setup** (gap-up, inverts, red H1, n=414): H2 lower low **57.3%** → **+13.6 pts** over baseline.
- **Bullish setup** (gap-down, inverts, green H1, n=490): H2 higher high **61.4%** → **+12.8 pts**.
- **2025–26 regime** (n=61): H2 lower low **63.9%** vs 40.0% baseline → **+23.9 pts**. Cleaner inversions in the current wide-gap regime.
- Edge shows up in the wick (lower low) more than the close (~48%, near coin flip).

Behaviour only — no strategy claim. Full breakdown on the study page.
