## Gap Inversion → Hourly Continuation

I took every NQ gap that inverts back through the prior close with a red first hour, 10 years (2016–2026, 33,739 hourly pairs), and checked what hour 2 does.

- Normal hour: lower low **43.6%** / higher high **48.6%**. That's the bar to beat.
- Gap-up, inverted, red H1 (414 nights): H2 lower low **57.3%** → **+13.6 pts**.
- Gap-down, inverted, green H1 (490): H2 higher high **61.4%** → **+12.8 pts**.
- 2025–26 (61 nights): H2 lower low **63.9%** vs 40.0% → **+23.9 pts**. Cleaner inversions in the wide-gap regime.
- Edge is in the wick, not the close (~48%, coin flip).

Not advice, AI-gathered data — backtest it yourself.
