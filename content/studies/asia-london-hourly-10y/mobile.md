## Asia → London — Does Asia Predict London?

The Asia→London handoff: which level London takes, how often it reverses, and whether Asia size predicts London. 10 years, 2,599 nights.

- London sweeps **Asia high 56.0%** / **Asia low 44.5%** of nights — upside lean. Both 13.5%, neither 13.0%.
- Turtle soup (sweep one extreme, break the other): bullish **6.3%**, bearish **7.2%** — rare.
- **Asia range tells you nothing about London**: London expands ≈ **42%** across every Asia quartile (Q1 42.2% / Q2 43.5% / Q3 41.9%).
- Stop sizing London off what Asia did.

Tendencies, not triggers. Not advice, AI data — backtest it.
