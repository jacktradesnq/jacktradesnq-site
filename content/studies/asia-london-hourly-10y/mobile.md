## Asia → London — Does Asia Predict London?

The Asia–London handoff: which level London sweeps, how often it reverses, and whether Asia range predicts London. 10-year study (2016–2026, 2,599 nights).

- London sweeps **Asia high 56.0%** / **Asia low 44.5%** of nights — upside lean. Both 13.5%, neither 13.0%.
- Turtle-soup reversal (sweep one extreme, break the other): bullish **6.3%**, bearish **7.2%** — rare.
- **Asia range does NOT predict London**: London expansion rate ≈ **42%** across every Asia-range quartile (Q1 42.2% / Q2 43.5% / Q3 41.9%).
