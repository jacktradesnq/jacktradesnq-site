## Asia → London — Does Asia Predict London? (NQ)

The Asia session sets the overnight range; London often runs it. This study looks at the Asia-to-London handoff three ways: which Asia level London sweeps more often, how often the sweep reverses (the turtle-soup setup), and whether the *size* of the Asia range carries any signal into London. Full 10-year history.

### Definition

- Instrument: NQ continuous front-month futures, 1-minute bars
- Period: 2016-01-01 → 2026-12-31
- Nights observed: **2,599**
- Asia killzone: 18:00–00:00 ET · post-Asia sweep window: 02:00–07:00 ET

### Which Asia level does London sweep?

Base rates over the 02:00–07:00 ET window:

- Sweeps the **Asia high**: **56.0%** of nights (high-only 42.5%)
- Sweeps the **Asia low**: **44.5%** of nights (low-only 31.0%)
- Sweeps **both**: 13.5%
- Sweeps **neither**: 13.0%

London leans to the upside — it takes the Asia high meaningfully more often than the Asia low, consistent with NQ's long-run drift. A clean single-side raid (one level, not both) happens about three nights in four.

### The turtle-soup reversal

The specific reversal setup — London sweeps one Asia extreme then breaks the *other* — is rare:

- Sweep Asia **low** first, then break Asia **high** (bullish turtle soup): **6.3%** of nights
- Sweep Asia **high** first, then break Asia **low** (bearish turtle soup): **7.2%** of nights

The textbook "raid one side, reverse to the other" night happens roughly one time in fifteen per direction. It is a real pattern, not a frequent one — most sweeps continue rather than fully reverse across the box.

### Does a big Asia range mean a big London range?

Splitting nights into quartiles by Asia range and measuring London's expansion rate:

| Asia range quartile | Mean Asia range | London expansion rate |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 (smallest) | 18.0 pts | 42.2% |
| Q2 | 42.9 pts | 43.5% |
| Q3 | 75.3 pts | 41.9% |

London's expansion rate sits near **42% regardless of how big or small Asia was**. A tight Asia does not foreshadow a tight London, and a wide Asia does not foreshadow a wide one — the Asia range carries essentially **no predictive signal** for London expansion. Each session sets its own range.

### Context

These are base rates and a non-result. "Asia doesn't predict London" is as useful as the sweep numbers — it tells you to stop sizing London off the Asia range. The 56/44 high-vs-low lean and the ~7% turtle-soup rate describe tendency, not a trade trigger. Behaviour only.
