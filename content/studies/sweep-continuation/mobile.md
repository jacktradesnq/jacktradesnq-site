## Sweep → Continuation or Retrace?

A wick sweeps a pivot then closes wrong-side — does it keep running or come back to re-tag the level first? I checked every NQ sweep over 10 years (52,355, 5-min bars).

- **Retrace first: 83.5%** (the norm) · immediate continuation **8.9%** · neither 7.2%.
- Continuation eventually shows up **61.3%** within 6h.
- By session — Asia: 81.4% / cont-6h 42.6%. London: 85.2% / 63.2%. NY AM: highest immediate (9.0%).
- Caveat: retrace target is close, continuation target is far (3× ATR), so the absolute 83.5% is inflated — read the *relative* gaps.
- What makes a sweep run immediately = how much the sweep bar displaced.

Not advice, AI data — backtest it.
