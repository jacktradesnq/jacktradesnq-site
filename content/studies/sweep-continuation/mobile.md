## Sweep → Continuation or Retrace?

Wick sweeps a recent pivot then closes wrong-side — does it continue or retrace to re-tag the level first? 10-year study (2016–2026, 52,355 sweeps, 5-min bars).

- **Retrace first: 83.5%** (the norm) · immediate continuation **8.9%** · neither 7.2%.
- Continuation eventually arrives **61.3%** within 6h.
- By session — Asia: retrace 81.4% / cont-6h 42.6%. London: 85.2% / 63.2%. NY AM: highest immediate cont (9.0%).
- Caveat: retrace target is close, continuation target is far (3× ATR), so absolute retrace is inflated — compare *relative*, not the raw 83.5%.
- Biggest driver of immediate runs = sweep-bar displacement.
