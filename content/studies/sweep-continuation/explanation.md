## Sweep → Continuation or Retrace? (NQ)

A wick takes out a recent high or low, then closes back the wrong side. Does NQ keep running, or come back to the level first?

`10 years · 52,355 sweeps · 5-min bars · 2016–2026`

**Retrace first — 83.5%.** Coming back to re-tag the swept level is the default by a mile.

- Immediate continuation (clean run, no pullback): **8.9%**
- Continuation reached within 6h: **61.3%**

### By session

| Session | Retrace first | Immediate | Within 6h |
|---|---|---|---|
| Asia | 81.4% | 6.3% | 42.6% |
| London | 85.2% | 5.0% | 63.2% |
| NY AM | 83.5% | 9.0% | highest |

### How to use it

- Expect the re-tag before the run. Immediate continuation is the **9% exception**.
- What separates the sweeps that run immediately = the size of the sweep bar's displacement.
- London re-tags the most but reaches continuation within 6h the most too (63.2%) — slow re-tag, then go.
- Caveat: retrace target is close, continuation target is far (3× ATR), so 83.5% is mechanically inflated. Compare sessions, not the absolute.
