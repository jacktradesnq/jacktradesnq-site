## CPI Week — Which Days Actually Move? (NQ)

People say the whole CPI week trades hot. I never believed every day runs hot, so I tested it — each weekday's range and how clean it trends, in weeks where CPI prints on Wednesday vs genuinely quiet weeks. 10 years of NQ.

### How I set it up

- NQ continuous front-month, 08:00–16:00 ET window
- 2016-04-20 → 2026-05-11
- 46 CPI-Wednesday weeks · 179 quiet weeks
- Range % = daily (high−low) ÷ open × 100, normalized for drift
- Efficiency = |close−open| ÷ range. Near 1 = clean trend, near 0 = choppy round-trip
- Quiet week = no CPI/PPI/NFP/FOMC/PCE/Retail/ADP/GDP/JOLTS/etc. on Mon, Tue or Wed

### The three claims, tested

| Claim | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Monday runs hot in CPI week | **False** in this regime |
| Tuesday is choppy | **True** |
| Wednesday is explosive | **True** — strongest signal |

### Wednesday — the real one

Wednesday (print day) is the only day that clearly expands:

- Range **+20.6%** vs a quiet Wednesday over 10 years (1.491% vs 1.236%).
- **+27.7%** in the recent 2023–2026 regime (1.623% vs 1.271%).
- But efficiency barely changes (~0.44 vs ~0.50) — bigger amplitude, **not** more directional. The print blows out the range, it doesn't make the day trend cleaner.

In points the gap is big and consistent: CPI Wednesday out-ranged the quiet Wednesday in 7 of the last 9 years, by as much as +144 pts (2022) and +135 pts (2024).

### Tuesday — choppy, not bigger

Tuesday's range is basically the same as a quiet Tuesday (ratio ~1.0), but its efficiency drops to **0.84× quiet** over 10 years and **0.74×** recently. It moves just as much, it just round-trips instead of trending. Pre-CPI positioning churns the tape without committing.

### Monday — doesn't lift anymore

Monday shows +9% range over the full 10 years, but in 2023–2026 the ratio is **0.98×** — CPI week doesn't lift Monday at all now. The "hot all week" idea is from an older regime.

So the energy is Wednesday — and even that is amplitude, not direction (efficiency flat). Tuesday chops, Monday's flat. That's where to look in a CPI week, not a reason to trade any one day.
