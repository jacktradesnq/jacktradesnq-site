## CPI Week — Which Days Actually Move? (NQ)

There's a belief that the whole week around a CPI release trades hot. This study tests it day by day: it compares each weekday's range and trend-efficiency in weeks where CPI prints on Wednesday against genuinely quiet weeks (no major data Mon/Tue/Wed), over 10 years of NQ.

### Definition

- Instrument: NQ continuous front-month futures, 08:00–16:00 ET window
- Period: 2016-04-20 → 2026-05-11
- **CPI-Wednesday weeks**: 46 · **quiet weeks**: 179
- **Range %**: daily (high−low) ÷ open × 100, normalized for price drift
- **Efficiency**: |close−open| ÷ range. Near 1 = clean trend; near 0 = choppy round-trip
- "Quiet week" = no CPI/PPI/NFP/FOMC/PCE/Retail/ADP/GDP/JOLTS/etc. on Mon, Tue or Wed

### The three claims, tested

| Claim | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Monday runs hot in CPI week | **False** in current regime |
| Tuesday is choppy | **True** |
| Wednesday is explosive | **True** — strongest signal |

### Wednesday — the real signal

Wednesday (the print day) is the one day that clearly expands:

- Range **+20.6%** vs a quiet Wednesday over 10 years (1.491% vs 1.236%).
- **+27.7%** in the recent 2023–2026 regime (1.623% vs 1.271%).
- But efficiency is unchanged (~0.44 vs ~0.50) — bigger amplitude, **not** more directional. The print expands the range; it doesn't make the day trend cleaner.

In points, the gap is large and persistent: the CPI Wednesday out-ranged the quiet Wednesday in 7 of the last 9 years, by as much as +144 pts (2022) and +135 pts (2024).

### Tuesday — choppy, not bigger

Tuesday's range is essentially the same as a quiet Tuesday (ratio ~1.0), but its efficiency collapses to **0.84× quiet** over 10 years and **0.74×** recently. It moves just as much — it just round-trips instead of trending. Pre-CPI positioning churns the tape without committing.

### Monday — no longer lifts

Monday shows a +9% range over the full 10 years, but in the 2023–2026 regime the ratio is **0.98×** — CPI week no longer lifts Monday at all. The "hot all week" idea is a relic of an earlier regime.

### Context

These are range and efficiency comparisons, not a setup. "Wednesday expands, Tuesday chops, Monday is flat" tells you *where the energy concentrates* in a CPI week — and crucially that the expansion is amplitude, not direction (efficiency flat). It is not a signal to trade any particular day. Behaviour only.
