## CPI Week — Which Days Actually Move?

People say the whole CPI week runs hot. I tested it day by day over 10 years — CPI-Wednesday weeks (46) vs quiet weeks (179), NQ 08:00–16:00 ET.

- **Wednesday explodes**: range **+20.6%** vs quiet Wed (10y), **+27.7%** in 2023–26. But efficiency flat (~0.44) — amplitude, not direction.
- **Tuesday choppy**: same range, efficiency drops to **0.84× quiet** (0.74× recent) — round-trips instead of trending.
- **Monday doesn't lift anymore**: 0.98× in 2023–26. "Hot all week" is from an older regime.
- CPI Wed out-ranged quiet Wed 7 of last 9 years (+144 pts in 2022).

Where the energy is, not a trade. Not advice, AI data — backtest it.
