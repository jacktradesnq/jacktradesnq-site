## Asia V-Reversal — When It Happens

I counted how often NQ carves a real V-reversal in the Asia session (18:00–00:00 ET) and what hour the turn lands. 10 years, 2,577 nights.

- **51.6%** of nights had a real V-reversal (down-leg ≥0.15% then rally ≥0.15%, low not in first 3 min).
- Median rally out of the low: **46 pts**.
- Hour (2h blocks ET): 18–20h **34.4%**, **20–22h 45.7%** (busy), 22–00h **19.8%**.
- Busiest hour: **20:00 ET** (23.7%), 21:00 ET close (22.0%). Over by 23:00.
- Window to watch ≈ 20:00–22:00 ET.

A clean V is ~a coin flip per night, and the sweep-low has to hold first. Not advice, AI data — backtest it.
