## Asia V-Reversal — When It Happens

How often NQ carves a genuine V-reversal in the Asia session (18:00–00:00 ET) and when the turn clusters. 10-year study (2016–2026, 2,577 nights).

- **51.6%** of nights had a genuine V-reversal (down-leg ≥0.15% then rally ≥0.15%, low not in first 3 min).
- Median rally out of the low: **46 pts**.
- Timing (2h blocks ET): 18–20h **34.4%**, **20–22h 45.7%** (dominant), 22–00h **19.8%**.
- Busiest single hour: **20:00 ET** (23.7% of reversals), 21:00 ET close (22.0%).
- Hour-clustering explains **78.6%** of reversals.

Behaviour only — a clean V is ~a coin flip per night. Full breakdown on the study page.
