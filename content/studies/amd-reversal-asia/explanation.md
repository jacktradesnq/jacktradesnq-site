## Asia V-Reversal — When It Happens (NQ)

The Asia session is where NQ often runs liquidity one way and then reverses for the rest of the night — the accumulation-manipulation-distribution pattern traders watch for. This study counts how often a *genuine* V-reversal forms between 18:00 and 00:00 ET, and times the sweep-low that anchors it, over the full 10-year history.

### Definition

- Instrument: NQ continuous front-month futures, 1-minute bars
- Period: 2016-04-19 → 2026-05-12
- Nights observed: **2,577**
- **Genuine V-reversal**: a down-leg of ≥0.15% into a low, then a rally of ≥0.15% back out — with the low *not* occurring in the first 3 minutes (filters opening noise)
- The "manipulation" reference is the sweep-low itself

### How often

- **1,330 nights** carried a genuine V-reversal — **51.6%** of all Asia sessions.
- Median rally out of the low: **46 points**.
- The hour-clustering below accounts for **78.6%** of all reversals (the rest scatter into the thin late-night hours).

Just over half of nights produce a clean down-then-up. The other half either trend straight, chop sideways, or invert (up-then-down) — so a V-reversal is the base case, not a certainty.

### When the turn clusters (2-hour blocks, ET)

Among the 1,330 reversal nights:

- **18:00–20:00 ET** (00:00–02:00 Paris): **34.4%** of reversals (n=458)
- **20:00–22:00 ET** (02:00–04:00 Paris): **45.7%** of reversals (n=608) — the dominant block
- **22:00–00:00 ET** (04:00–06:00 Paris): **19.8%** of reversals (n=264)

The single busiest hour is **20:00 ET** (23.7% of all reversals), with 21:00 ET close behind (22.0%). The turn most often lands in the middle of the Asia session, not at the open and not at the very end. The late block thins out fast — by 23:00 ET only 6.5% of reversals are still forming.

### Context

This describes timing and frequency, not a setup. A 51.6% reversal rate means a clean V is roughly a coin flip on any given night, and the 20:00–22:00 cluster tells you *where to look*, not *what to do*. The sweep-low has to actually form and hold before any of this matters. Behaviour only — no edge promise.
