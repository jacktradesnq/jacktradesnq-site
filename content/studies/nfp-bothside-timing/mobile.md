## NFP — Both-Side Sweep Timing

I timed how often NQ sweeps both sides of the pre-NFP range, and whether it's done before the 09:30 cash open. 10 years, 121 NFP days. Ref range = 08:15–08:30 ET.

- **Both sides swept same day: 81.8%** of NFP days.
- Of those, **73.7%** done **before 09:30** (premarket).
- First side taken pre-9:30 on **100%** of both-side days.
- Getting earlier: 2025+ → 83.3% done pre-9:30.
- The range gets taken at 08:30, not the cash open.

Tells you when the chop comes, not the direction. Not advice, AI data — backtest it.
