## NFP — Both-Side Sweep Timing

How often NQ sweeps both sides of the pre-NFP range, and whether it's done before the 09:30 cash open. 10-year study (2016–2026, 121 NFP days). Ref range = 08:15–08:30 ET.

- **Both sides swept same day: 81.8%** of NFP days.
- Of those, **73.7%** complete **before 09:30** (premarket reaction).
- First side taken pre-9:30 on **100%** of both-side days.
- Sharpening: 2025+ → 83.3% complete pre-9:30.
- The 08:30 print, not the cash open, is where the range gets taken.

Behaviour only — both-side ≠ directional edge. Full breakdown on the study page.
