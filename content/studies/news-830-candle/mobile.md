## The 8:30 Candle — First Move & Reversal

That 1-min candle at 08:30 ET on a news drop: how often the other side gets taken and how long it takes. 10 years, 1,166 days (FOMC left out).

- **Other side almost always taken**: CPI 73% · NFP 70% · PPI 75% · PCE 82% · Jobless Claims **86.6%**.
- **Bigger impact = wider candle, slower flip**: CPI/NFP print widest candles (60–72 pts), slowest back to the other side (42–62 min). Jobless Claims = 12-pt candle, 18 min.
- Close confirms high everywhere (79–92%) — first move is real, just rarely the only one.

Round-trip tendency, not direction. Also on ES/GC/SI in the hub. Not advice, AI data — backtest it.
