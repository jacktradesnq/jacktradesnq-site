## The 8:30 Candle — First Move & Reversal

The 1-min candle at 08:30 ET on a news release: how often the opposite side gets taken and how long it takes. 10-year study (2016–2026, 1,166 days, FOMC excluded).

- **Other side almost always taken**: CPI 73% · NFP 70% · PPI 75% · PCE 82% · Jobless Claims **86.6%**.
- **Impact scales it**: CPI/NFP print widest candles (60–72 pts), slowest to revisit other side (42–62 min). Jobless Claims = 12-pt candle, 18 min round-trip.
- Close confirms high everywhere (79–92%) — first move is real, just rarely the only move.

Behaviour only — round-trip tendency, not direction. Also on ES/GC/SI in the hub. Full table on the study page.
