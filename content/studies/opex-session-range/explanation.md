## Opex Week — Which Sessions Compress? (NQ)

Options-expiration week — the Monday-to-Friday around the third Friday — has a reputation for pinning price and squeezing volatility. This study tests that across 10 years of NQ, session by session, to see whether the compression is real and where it actually shows up.

### Definition

- Instrument: NQ continuous front-month futures
- Period: 2016-04-19 → 2026-12-31
- Sessions observed: **7,748**
- **Opex week**: Monday–Friday of the third-Friday week
- Range measured in NQ points per session; also ATR-normalized (session range ÷ its own trailing 14-session average) to strip out regime drift
- Sessions: Asia (18:00–00:00 ET), London (02:00–05:00 ET), NY AM (09:30–11:00 ET)
- Contract-count data omitted — contaminated by the quarterly contract roll that coincides with Opex

### Range: Opex vs the rest of the month

| Session | Opex avg range | Non-Opex avg range | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Asia | 62.5 pts | 72.3 pts | **−13.6%** |
| London | 61.6 pts | 62.1 pts | **−0.8%** |
| NY AM | 113.1 pts | 120.7 pts | **−6.3%** |

The compression is real but uneven. **Asia** is the most pinned session during Opex week, losing nearly 14% of its range. **NY AM** compresses modestly (−6.3%). **London is essentially untouched** — its range during Opex is statistically the same as any other week (−0.8%).

### The London exception

London is the one session that does *not* compress during Opex. While the overnight Asia book sits on its hands waiting for expiration and the US cash session trades a little tighter, the London window keeps delivering its normal range. In a week where most of the tape is supposed to be quiet, London is where movement still shows up — a regime feature that held across 2022–2025.

### Context

These are average and median ranges, not a setup. "London doesn't compress in Opex" is an observation about *where* range survives, not a signal to trade it — a normal-range session can still be directionless. Volume confirmation isn't available here (contract roll contaminates it). Behaviour only.
