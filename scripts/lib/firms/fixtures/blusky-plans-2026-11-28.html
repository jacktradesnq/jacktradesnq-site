Common questions

Answers to the questions traders ask most before starting.

Can I really request a payout every day?

Yes, if you meet the criteria and your account balance is eligible, you can request a payout every day.

Does the monthly evaluation fee go away after I pass my evaluation?

Yes once you pass the evaluation the subscription ends, but that fee is non-refundable and applies regardless of the outcome of your evaluation. It covers the cost of reviewing your progress.

Are there ANY fees after I pass my evaluation?

Launch plans have a one-time Launch fee, due when you pass the evaluation. For all other plans, there are no additional fees after passing the evaluation. However, there may be transaction or brokerage fees depending on your activities.

Am I risking any of my own funds at the brokerage?

No, you are not risking your personal funds. The brokerage provides the capital for trading, but there may be performance-based fees or splits.

If I’m doing well at the brokerage, can I get more contracts?

Yes, if you’re consistently performing well, you may qualify for additional contracts and more capital to trade.

Why should I go with BluSky over the other prop firms?

BluSky offers competitive terms, flexible evaluation processes, and transparent fee structures. Our personalized coaching and support make us stand out in the industry.

Can I use TradingView?

Yes, TradingView can be used for technical analysis, and many traders prefer it for its robust charting tools.

What are the rules in funded?

The rules vary depending on your contract, but in general, you must maintain risk management standards, follow profit-sharing agreements, and adhere to trading guidelines.

How does the FREE coaching work?

Coaching is available daily on our live YouTube streams, where we review trading strategies, address questions, and provide guidance to improve your performance.
