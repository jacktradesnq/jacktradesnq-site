Common Questions

What traders ask most before they start their first challenge

MyFundedPerps is a perpetuals-focused prop firm. We give traders access to up to $300K in total funded allocation to trade perpetuals. Pass our challenge, prove your edge, and trade on our platform from the team behind My Funded Futures.

Maximum $300K total funded allocation, with a maximum of $100K per model type. If another passed account would exceed either limit, it enters Funding Review instead of being automatically funded.

Reach the profit target for your selected plan while respecting its daily and maximum loss limits. Check your dashboard for the account’s rules and inactivity deadline.

Crypto, stocks, commodities, indices, and forex perpetuals with real-time order books. Crypto trades 24/7; other markets follow scheduled sessions.

Once funded, you keep the profit share purchased with your account. You can submit one payout request per funded account per New York calendar day, subject to eligibility and review. Elite accounts have no daily limit.

The full gross payout is deducted from the funded account and a new payout cycle begins. On Select, Signature, and Prime, profit retained below the new cycle baseline becomes a balance buffer rather than profit available for another withdrawal. On Elite, profit you leave in the account stays available for a later payout.

On Select, Prime, and Signature, the first withdrawal locks the maximum loss limit (MLL) floor permanently to starting balance. Later withdrawals never move it. Equity exactly at that locked floor is allowed; falling below it breaches. The 3% daily loss rule still applies. Elite has no lock: its MLL floor never moves, and only its 3% DLL option has a daily loss limit.

For example, receiving $3,200 at an 80/20 split deducts $4,000 gross. Withdrawing all $4,000 of profit leaves no buffer above the locked MLL floor. Review Balance After Payout before confirming.

There is no general limit on how many challenges you can buy. Active evaluations are limited to $100,000 in total account size per model type. Elite No DLL purchases are also limited to $250,000 in total account size in any rolling 7-day period, across all Elite No DLL sizes combined; Elite 3% DLL is not affected by that limit. Each order also has a maximum number of accounts per account size.
